SECTION 1: Executive Overview & Public Production Deployments

HEATSHIELD AI is an authoritative, multi-agent urban heat resilience platform designed to safeguard municipal workforces, transit corridors, and high-density urban sectors from extreme heat events. The platform directly addresses the limitations of macro-level weather forecasts by ingesting high-resolution microclimate telemetry from the FortyGuard Temperature API, calculating physiological heat stress indices (ISO 7243 WBGT, NOAA Heat Index, UTCI), and driving automated multi-agent operational mitigation workflows.

SECTION 3: UI Guardrails & Design Constraints

To ensure rock-solid production stability and eliminate cognitive clutter, HEATSHIELD enforces the following strict frontend UI constraints:

🔒 1. Strict 2-Option Model Dropdown

The model selector dropdown menu on the chat toolbar has been strictly restricted to list ONLY TWO platform engines. Extraneous options (e.g. Gemini 3.7 Flash High, Gemini 2.5 Pro, FortyGuard Neural-v2) have been removed:

  1. V1 Stable — Baseline Heat Analytics Engine
  2. V2 Thermora — Dynamic AI Heat Intelligence Engine
🎙️ 2. Microphone & Audio Icon Elimination

The microphone/voice recording icon (🎙) has been completely removed across both version interfaces to maintain a focused, command-line text interface.

✨ 3. Asterisk & Star Sanitization Protocol

To prevent messy markdown bolding and asterisks in technical reports, HEATSHIELD enforces a dual-layer sanitization protocol:

  • Backend System Instruction: "Do not use markdown bolding, asterisks (*), or star decorators anywhere in your output. Return clean, unformatted plain text with plain numbers and line breaks."
  • Frontend Parser Sanitizer: All incoming messages pass through cleanedText = rawText.replace(/\*/g, '') before DOM insertion.
