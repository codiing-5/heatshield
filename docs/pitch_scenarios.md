# HEATSHIELD — Hackathon Pitch Kit & Live Demonstration Guide

---

## 🎙️ 3-Minute Live Hackathon Pitch Script

### 1. The Problem (0:00 – 0:45)
> "Extreme urban heat is no longer a future projection—it is an acute, compounding crisis claiming more lives annually than hurricanes, floods, and tornadoes combined. 
> 
> Cities face two fatal blindspots:
> 1. **Coarse Weather Feeds:** Standard regional forecasts report ambient airport temperatures (e.g., 38°C), failing to detect microclimate surface asphalt temperatures exceeding **48°C to 52°C**.
> 2. **Fragmented, Reactive Responses:** Public health, power grids, emergency response, and construction safety operate in disconnected silos. By the time emergency rooms fill up, it is too late."

### 2. The Solution: HEATSHIELD (0:45 – 1:30)
> "Welcome to **HEATSHIELD**—the autonomous Agentic AI Heat Intelligence Platform powered by the **FortyGuard Temperature API**.
> 
> HEATSHIELD ingests hyper-local street-level surface and air telemetry, calculates scientific biometeorological stress (ISO 7243 WBGT, UTCI, NOAA Heat Index), predicts 24-hour microclimate trajectories with ML, and deploys **4 specialized autonomous AI agents** to synthesize and dispatch immediate cross-domain interventions."

### 3. Live Demonstration Walkthrough (1:30 – 2:30)
> *(Demonstrate live platform views)*
> 1. **Command Center:** Real-time FortyGuard surface telemetry vs. ambient air temperature and active heat stress advisories.
> 2. **MapLibre GL Geospatial Map:** Dark vector GIS map rendering dynamic GeoJSON thermal polygons and pulsing IoT sensor pins.
> 3. **Predictive Analytics Sandbox:** Neural microclimate forecasts (+1h to +24h) and interactive simulation sliders testing high-albedo coatings, misting arrays, and tree canopies.
> 4. **Agentic AI Studio:** Autonomous multi-agent cycle (Sentinel, Vulnerable Advisor, Cooling Strategist, Grid Balancer) with transparent step-by-step reasoning chains, tool execution times, and conversational AI assistant.
> 5. **Seven-Track Domain Hub:** Direct tactical dispatchers enforcing labor rest rotations, routing elderly cooling shelters, and buffering power grids.

### 4. The Impact & Vision (2:30 – 3:00)
> "HEATSHIELD transforms urban heat management from delayed emergency response into **predictive, autonomous, evidence-grounded urban resilience**. 
> 
> With FortyGuard microclimate intelligence and agentic automation, we protect vulnerable lives, preserve energy infrastructure, and ensure climate-resilient cities."
